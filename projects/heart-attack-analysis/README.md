# Heart Attack History Among Men (DATA 550)

HTML report from my final project for DATA 550 (Data Science Toolkit) at Emory's Rollins School of Public Health, published here so it can be opened from my portfolio.

- **View the report:** https://mariaalvarez95.github.io/projects/heart-attack-analysis/report.html
- **Code and reproducible workflow (R Markdown, Make, renv, Docker):** https://github.com/MariaAlvarez95/final_project_maria

The report describes male heads of household in the Panel Study of Income Dynamics who reported a heart attack: a descriptive table of their characteristics and the distribution of age at first heart attack.
